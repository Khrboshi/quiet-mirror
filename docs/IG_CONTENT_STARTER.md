## Visual direction

Use Quiet Mirror’s existing homepage palette and typography. Keep compositions spacious, low-pressure, and legible on mobile. Prefer real product UI, abstract texture, and fictional journal examples over stock lifestyle imagery or claims about transformation.

## First twelve posts

1. **Prompt:** “What did you carry today that nobody saw?” CTA: Save this for tonight.
2. **Product loop:** Journal → reflection → Insights. CTA: See the existing homepage.
3. **Prompt:** “What felt easy today, and why?” CTA: Write one honest sentence.
4. **Trust:** Quiet Mirror notices patterns; it does not diagnose you. CTA: Learn what it is built to do.
5. **Fictional example:** Show a routine entry becoming a grounded reflection. Label it fictional.
6. **Privacy:** Explain that private journal text should not be used as marketing proof. CTA: Read the product promise.
7. **Prompt:** “Where did your time go today?” CTA: Notice, do not judge.
8. **Insights:** Explain that patterns become more useful as entries accumulate. Do not promise instant clarity.
9. **Early access:** Full access at no charge while Quiet Mirror is being shaped. CTA: Try the existing homepage.
10. **Build note:** Share a real quality improvement, such as cautious sparse-data Insights or localization QA.
11. **Language:** Introduce the supported language experience without claiming native-level perfection.
12. **Prompt:** “What would you like to remember about today?” CTA: Start with one entry.

## Caption rules

- Use calm, concrete language.
- Avoid diagnosis, treatment, therapy, crisis-resolution, and guaranteed-outcome claims.
- Never use QA data, fictional entries, or invented testimonials as social proof.
- Do not promote Premium pricing or payments while early access is active.
- Every product post should link to the existing homepage.

## Measurement

Use Instagram UTM tracking and record profile visits, link clicks, signup completion, first journal entry, reflection received, and 3–7 day return. Do not scale reach until the clean internal retention test passes.
